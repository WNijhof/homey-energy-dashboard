Energy Dashboard turns any screen in your home into a live energy display. Homey serves the dashboard as a web page on your own network, so a wall tablet, laptop or phone only needs a browser. No extra computer, cloud account or API key is needed.

The dashboard is built from blocks you arrange yourself:

- Energy now: live flow between solar, grid, home and home battery, with moving particles
- Power today: the power through the day, showing solar self-consumption, grid import and export
- Energy flows: a Sankey chart from your sources to the individual devices, live or for a period
- Totals, electricity, solar, gas and water charts per day, week, month or year
- Hot water: temperature and estimated shower minutes of your water heater
- Costs with your own contract: fixed (normal and off-peak) or dynamic (market price per hour plus tax and your supplier's markup), gas, water and fixed costs
- Electricity prices from Homey Energy for any country (with the costs you entered in Homey), or from EnergyZero for the Netherlands
- Battery use: when your home battery charged or delivered, from the sun or the grid, and at what price
- Monthly peak for the Belgian capacity tariff, with a warning when a quarter hour goes above it
- Solar forecast for as many roof planes as you have, heating, EV charger, standby use, and phase load through the day

New: look back at any moment of today or yesterday to see which device used what; what the end of net metering (salderen) in 2027 costs you, based on your own data; what your home battery earns; solar performance against the forecast; gas per degree day; warnings for devices left on and rising standby use; prices per quarter hour; a layout per screen; CSV export; install on your home screen.

Two widgets for Homey Dashboards: "Energy now" with the live flow between solar, grid, home and battery, and "Energy flows" from your sources through your rooms to the devices using the most power.

For a tablet on the wall: full screen, keep the screen on, and a night mode that dims the screen or turns it black.

Devices are found automatically from Homey Energy, and you can choose them yourself in the app settings. The dashboard works in light and dark mode, on any screen size, in Dutch, English, German, Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, Italian, French, Portuguese and Spanish.

Open the app settings to find the address of the dashboard. You can protect it with an access code, and the layout with a PIN.
